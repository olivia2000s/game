Settings

Privacy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

Elevate is built to be private by default. Here's exactly how your information is handled.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. Everything you create — your dog, names, XP, level, streak, owned items, and the activity numbers you log (steps, workout, sleep, water, and so on) — is stored only in this app's local storage on your own device.

No accounts, no tracking

There are no user accounts, no servers we control, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. Your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup file yourself.

Health information

Any health-related numbers are values you type in manually, and they stay on your device with everything else. This version does not automatically read from Apple Health or Google Fit; the "connect" buttons simply open those apps so you can copy your numbers in.

Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and off until you enable them. They are local notifications generated on your device — you can turn them off any time in Settings.

No third-party services

The app makes no network requests. The fonts are bundled inside the app, so nothing is fetched from any server and everything works fully offline.

Your control

You can back up, restore, or permanently erase all of your data at any time from Settings. Erasing removes it from this device.

Children

Elevate collects no personal information and is suitable for a general audience.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated. Questions: supportelevate@proton.me.
